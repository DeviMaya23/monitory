Remove a target (proposed: inline confirm)deleteTarget

The brief leaves this open. Proposal: no dialog; Remove turns the row’s actions into “Remove this target? Cancel / Remove” in place, with focus on Remove. Enter confirms, Esc or Cancel restores the buttons. After removal focus moves to the next row’s Edit button, or to Add target. A toast confirms.
